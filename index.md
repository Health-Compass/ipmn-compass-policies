# IPMN Compass — Policies

Publicly-hosted policy documents for the IPMN Compass mobile application
(iOS + Android). Required by Apple App Privacy and Google Play Data
Safety. The canonical source lives in the private
[`Health-Compass/IPMN-Compass`](https://github.com/Health-Compass/IPMN-Compass)
repo at `docs/policies/`; these files mirror that content for public
fetching by app-store review systems.

## Policies

- [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) — what we collect, how it is
  stored, and your rights.
- [Account & Data Deletion](data-deletion.html) — how to request
  deletion of your IPMN Compass account and associated data.

## About IPMN Compass

IPMN Compass is a clinic-visit companion for clinicians and patients
navigating intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasm (IPMN) management
decisions. It is in pre-MVP development.

Co-built by Jason Castellanos MD (Fox Chase Cancer Center) and Greg
Sacks MD MPH PhD (NYU Langone, hepato-pancreato-biliary surgical
oncology), operationalizing Greg's NCI K08CA293277 — "Optimizing
surgical decision-making for premalignant pancreatic cysts."

For questions about these policies: `jason.castellanos@gmail.com`.
